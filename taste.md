# My taste

I'm a Princeton ECE senior (chip design + AI/ML hardware) who reads a lot of academic papers and class material, so I want the digest to be what I don't already get: interesting, well-written pieces that sit between a paper and a casual post.

**I want:**
- AI/ML: new ideas, how models work, thoughtful takes. Not just release news.
- Math and physics at the "cool idea" level. Not textbook-level derivations or proofs, and not core physics I already know.
- Essays and short fiction (Emerson-style essays, New Yorker stories), literary and philosophical writing, and narrative history.
- A strong voice and a real idea.

**I don't want:** summaries, hype, news, product announcements, clickbait.

**How to pick:** the 5 posts most worth reading today, each with a one-line reason to read it. Favor depth over recency. Reserve one slot as a wildcard: the best piece of the day from outside these interests (any field, any style) that I'd likely never have looked for.
